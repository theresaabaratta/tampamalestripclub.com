ABOUT THIS DIRECTORY

Built for one job: finding a good show in Tampa.

We list the clubs, agencies, and booking routes for male exotic entertainment in Tampa, FL, so you spend less time guessing and more time planning the night itself.

WHY THIS EXISTS

Tampa's male revue scene is smaller and harder to search than the female club scene.

Search "male strip club Tampa" and you'll mostly find dead directories, private-party dispatch services mislabeled as clubs, and a lot of outdated Yellow Pages listings. This site exists to cut through that: one page for the region's continuous male revue show, the agencies that book private male entertainment, and honest guidance on the independent-dancer side of the market.

We're not a talent agency ourselves. We link out to the venues and services that do the actual booking, and we keep the directory current as shows, agencies, and contact details change.

How a listing gets added

A business gets listed here if it:

  • Actively operates or books male exotic entertainment in the Tampa Bay area
  • Has a working, current contact method (site, phone, or social page)
  • Confirms it's 18+ only, no exceptions
