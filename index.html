GOING INDEPENDENT

Booking an independent dancer

Outside of Tampa's club shows, a lot of male entertainment is booked directly through freelance performers or general event-entertainment marketplaces. We couldn't verify a stable roster of named independent Tampa performers worth listing individually — that side of the market turns over fast — so here's how to find and vet one yourself. We support dancer and groups like Florida, Poison Exotic Entertainment, and those abroad like Silver Fox, Bailey Exotic Dancer & Michael At Your Place who all are amazing male strippers

1

Search a booking marketplace

Sites like GigSalad and The Bash list vetted entertainers who serve the Tampa area, with reviews and verified booking counts attached to each profile.

2

Check reviews & photos

Look for real client reviews, recent event photos, and a clear rate sheet. A performer with no verifiable history is a much bigger risk for a deposit.

3

Confirm the terms up front

Get set length, arrival time, what's included, and total cost in writing before any money changes hands — the same rule that applies to booking any club show.

A note on safety: only pay deposits through traceable methods, meet in a public or well-lit venue for events when possible, and never send full payment before the performer confirms details in writing.
